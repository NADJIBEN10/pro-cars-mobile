**Project Name:** Rahand Cars

**Status:** Active Development

**Platform:** React Native + Expo

**Target Platforms:** Android & iOS

**Repository:** https://github.com/NADJIBEN10/pro-cars-mobile

---

# 🎯 Vision

Rahand Cars aims to become the leading automotive marketplace in Iraq and Kurdistan by connecting private sellers, professional dealers, showrooms, and buyers in one premium mobile application.

The application will provide a modern, secure, scalable, and AI-powered platform designed for long-term business growth.
